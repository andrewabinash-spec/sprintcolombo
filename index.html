01 — Strategic positioning

IRONMAN is the international stage. Sprint Colombo is Colombo's challenge.

A festival for everyone

Energetic and accessible, attracting experienced endurance athletes and people trying multisport for the first time.

Backed by Serendib

Serendib Multisport's event credibility builds trust and connects Sprint Colombo to the wider endurance community.

Local and achievable

Local, energetic, social and achievable, with the quality and excitement of a major sporting event.

13 — How we work

Our weekly workflow.

An eight-week campaign moves fast, so we run the cycle weekly rather than monthly.

Step 1Plan

Weekly content plan and shot list for the current phase, approved by Serendib.

Step 2Shoot

Teaser, launch, training and activation shoots, with our team on site.

Step 3Review

Edits shared for feedback and sign-off before anything goes live.

Step 4Publish

Posting, creator and partner collabs, community replies, paid boosts where agreed.

Step 5Report

Weekly registrations by creator link and content results, with next week's changes.

On the ground at key moments

Our team attends in person to shoot and cover live, subject to Serendib's requirements and approval.
